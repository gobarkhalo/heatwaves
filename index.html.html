Core SMP Rules & Guidelines

Fair play keeps the survival stakes real for everyone.

Use of hacked clients, X-Ray texture packs, auto-clickers, baritone, crystal macros, or duping exploits is strictly prohibited and monitored by our anti-cheat and active moderation team. Standard performance mods (Sodium, Lithium, Iris) are welcome.
Disconnecting during active PvP combat will result in an instant kill and full inventory drop. Do not attempt to bypass combat tags using server bugs or dimension portals when restricted.
Strategic raiding and faction warfare are core parts of Heat Waves SMP, but pointless terrain destruction (lavacasting spawn, lag machines, or chunk-banning) is strictly forbidden. Check the official Discord for full war declaration rules.
Keep rivalries in-character and inside the game. Hate speech, slurs, real-life threats, doxxing, or toxic harassment in global chat or Discord will result in an immediate permanent ban.
